Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Introduction

Guess It respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Guess It app and website.

Information We Collect

Guess It does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information during normal gameplay.

Game Data

Game settings, preferences, and progress may be stored locally on your device so the app can remember your choices between sessions.

Analytics

At this time Guess It does not use third-party analytics or advertising.

Children's Privacy

Guess It is designed to be family friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Links

Links to GitHub or support resources may take you away from the Guess It website. Those external services have their own privacy practices and policies.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit the Support page.
