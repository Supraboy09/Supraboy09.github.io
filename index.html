How It Works

Guess It is easy to learn and fun for everyone. Pick a category, hand the phone to a player, and see how many words you can guess before time runs out.

  1. 1 🎯

    Choose a Category

    Select from a variety of family-friendly categories.

  2. 2 📱

    Pass the Phone

    Place the phone on your forehead while everyone gives clues.

  3. 3 🏆

    Score Points

    Guess as many words as possible before the timer ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your next game night.

How many people can play?

Guess It is designed for groups of all sizes. Play with family, friends, classrooms, or parties.

Does Guess It work offline?

Yes. Once installed, Guess It can be played without an internet connection.

Is Guess It suitable for kids?

Absolutely. Guess It is designed to be fun and family friendly for players of all ages.

What game modes are included?

Classic Guess It and Two Truths & a Lie are included, with more ideas planned for future updates.

Will new categories be added?

Yes. We're continually expanding the library with new categories and fresh content.
